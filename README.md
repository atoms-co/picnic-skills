# Picnic Marketplace

Picnic is a food delivery platform designed for office meals. This plugin helps
you browse delivery windows, restaurants, and menus; choose meal options; manage
your cart; preview and place an order; and check your order history. Picnic
Delivery LLC publishes the plugin.

## Getting started

Connect the Picnic Marketplace MCP connector and sign in with your Picnic
account. Choose a default delivery hub and save a payment method on the
[Picnic website](https://trypicnic.com) before ordering. Your account must have
Picnic MCP access. Available menus, delivery windows, and account benefits come
from the connected service.

Try “Show me lunch options for tomorrow”, “Add this meal to my cart”, or “Show my
upcoming orders”. The plugin previews the order and asks you to approve the exact
total before placing it. Production orders charge your saved payment method.
Use the Picnic website for account settings and operations the connector does
not provide, including cancellation.

## Connection and data

The bundle contains instructions and a remote MCP connection definition. It does
not install or launch a local server. The connector sends tool requests to the
Picnic service as your signed-in account, including searches, cart selections,
and order requests. Dish images may be displayed through the connector's image
links or interactive cards. Optional feedback is sent to the Picnic team under
your name and email only according to your feedback consent. Read the supplied
skills for ordering and feedback consent details.

For help, visit [Picnic](https://trypicnic.com). See the
[privacy notice](https://trypicnic.com/legal/privacy-notice) and
[terms of service](https://trypicnic.com/legal/terms-of-service).
